Overview

About

JPMorgan Chase & Co. is a global financial-services firm headquartered in New York. It serves consumers, small businesses, corporations, governments, and institutions through a broad range of banking, payments, lending, investment, markets, and asset-management services.

The firm’s main businesses are Consumer & Community Banking, Commercial & Investment Banking, and Asset & Wealth Management, alongside its corporate functions. Its Commercial & Investment Bank provides strategic advice, capital raising, risk management, liquidity, payments, and securities services to clients globally.

JPMorgan Chase was formed in 2000 through the merger of J.P. Morgan & Co. and Chase Manhattan Corporation. Jamie Dimon has been its Chairman and Chief Executive Officer since 2006. The firm reported 318,512 employees globally as of 31 December 2025, operating in 66 countries.

Technology is central to the firm’s operations and client services. Its technology teams develop large-scale digital-banking, payments, data, cloud, cybersecurity, AI, and financial-markets platforms, supporting millions of customers and institutional clients worldwide.
