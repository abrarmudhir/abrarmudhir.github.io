      LSEG (London Stock Exchange Group) is a global financial markets infrastructure and data provider headquartered in London. Its purpose is to drive financial stability, empower economies, and enable customers to create sustainable growth.<br /><br />

      The Group's businesses include Data & Analytics, FTSE Russell, London Stock Exchange, FX, Post Trade, and Risk Intelligence. Together, they provide financial-market data and analytics, benchmark indices, trading and execution venues, clearing and settlement services, foreign-exchange solutions, and tools for risk and compliance.<br /><br />

      LSEG was formed in 2007 through the combination of the London Stock Exchange and Borsa Italiana, building on the London Stock Exchange's history dating to 1801. David Schwimmer has been Chief Executive Officer since 2018.<br /><br />

      The company employs more than 26,000 people across 65 countries. Its engineering teams build and operate real-time data platforms, ultra-low-latency trading systems, cloud-native services, cybersecurity capabilities, and AI-enabled products that support global financial markets.<br /><br />
