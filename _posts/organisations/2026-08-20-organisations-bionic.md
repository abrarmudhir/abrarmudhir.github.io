      Bionic is a London-based business-comparison platform that helps small and medium-sized UK businesses compare, switch, and renew essential services. Its offering spans commercial energy, insurance, broadband and phone services, VoIP, mobile connectivity, and business finance.<br /><br />

      The company combines data-led comparison tools with human advisers to identify relevant products from a vetted supplier panel and manage the switching process. Bionic says it has helped more than 200,000 British businesses since 2007 and provides business switching services for several major consumer-comparison brands.<br /><br />

      Bionic's platform and operations use business data to reduce form filling, support comparisons, coordinate supplier switching, and manage customer renewals. Paul Galligan is Chief Executive Officer and leads a group of more than 450 employees. Its technology, data, product, and operations teams support digital customer journeys, supplier integrations, analytics, and SME services platforms.
