      Standard Life is a UK retirement savings and income business with roots dating to 1825. Its consumer website supports personal, employer, and adviser customers with pension products, retirement planning tools, financial advice, investment information, and services for managing retirement savings.<br /><br />

      Standard Life states that it manages £300 billion in assets on behalf of 12 million customers and aims to help a further 3 million people achieve better retirement outcomes over the next decade. The business operates alongside Phoenix Life, ReAssure, SunLife, Phoenix Wealth, and Standard Life Corporate Investment Solutions within the wider Phoenix Group.<br /><br />

      The Standard Life website describes its customer-facing products and services as being provided by Phoenix Life Limited, trading as Standard Life. Phoenix Life Limited is registered in England and Wales and is authorised by the Prudential Regulation Authority and regulated by both the Financial Conduct Authority and Prudential Regulation Authority.<br /><br />
