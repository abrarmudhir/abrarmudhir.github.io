      Searchland is a London-based property technology company founded in 2020. Its map-based platform brings together planning, ownership, market, policy, and constraint data so users can identify and assess development opportunities across the United Kingdom.<br /><br />

      The platform supports developers, land agents, planners, architects, investors, commercial occupiers, and energy firms. Its tools include AI-powered site sourcing, planning-application and local-policy data, ownership mapping, residential and commercial comparables, title reports, site constraints, energy-network data, and tracked landowner outreach.<br /><br />

      Searchland states that more than 2,000 property professionals use its platform. The business was founded by Mitchell Fasanya, Hugh Canning Gibbs, Arthur Goodhart, and Archie Kennedy-Dyson; Mitchell Fasanya is Co-Founder and Chief Executive Officer. Its London headquarters is in Tagwright House, Westland Place.
