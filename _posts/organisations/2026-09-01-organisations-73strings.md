      73 Strings is a global fintech company that provides AI-powered valuation and portfolio-intelligence software for alternative asset managers. Its platform brings data extraction, portfolio monitoring and valuation workflows together in a single environment for private equity, private credit, venture capital, growth equity and infrastructure investors.<br /><br />

      Its products include 73 Extract for turning unstructured portfolio data into decision-ready information, 73 Monitor for portfolio intelligence and analytics, and 73 Value for digitalising valuation workflows. The company positions these capabilities as audit-ready tools for investment teams working with illiquid assets.<br /><br />
