      St. James’s Place (SJP) is a UK wealth-management company founded in 1991. It provides one-to-one financial advice through its network of advisers and Partner Practices, combining personalised financial planning with investment, retirement, protection, tax, mortgage, banking, and intergenerational wealth-management services.<br /><br />

      The company supports its adviser network with training, governance, product and investment support, and legal and financial resources. Its investment offering includes life, pension, unit-trust, and international funds, while its services are aimed at helping clients plan, grow, and protect their financial future.<br /><br />

      St. James’s Place plc is a FTSE 100 company headquartered in Cirencester, with offices including London and Aberdeen. Mark FitzPatrick has been Chief Executive Officer since December 2023. Its technology and operations teams support adviser and client digital services, data and analytics, investment platforms, cybersecurity, regulatory reporting, and enterprise systems.
