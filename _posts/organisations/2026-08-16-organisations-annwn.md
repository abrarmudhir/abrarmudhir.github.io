      Annwn is an AI-native application for sustainability professionals. It is designed to help teams spend less time on compliance and reporting work, and more time on sustainability initiatives with strategic and commercial impact.<br /><br />

      The platform helps organisations gather and reconcile data, coordinate with contributors, draft disclosures, and identify where professional judgement is needed. It combines an organisation's context with a curated corpus of sustainability knowledge to support evidence-based insight and decision-making.<br /><br />

      Annwn's initial use cases include CDP and UK Sustainability Reporting Standards (UK SRS) work, double-materiality assessments, ratings questionnaires, horizon scanning, sustainability investment cases, and board preparation. The company also aims to surface patterns relating to cost reduction, risk exposure, and commercial opportunity.<br /><br />
