      Bending Spoons is a Milan-based technology company that acquires and operates digital businesses. Following an acquisition, it invests in product technology, user experience, feature development, marketing, monetisation and organisational design to improve the business for long-term ownership.<br /><br />

      Its portfolio includes products and brands such as AOL, Brightcove, Eventbrite, Evernote, komoot, Meetup, Remini, StreamYard, Vimeo and WeTransfer. The company says its products have served more than one billion people worldwide.<br /><br />

      Founded in 2013, Bending Spoons is headquartered in Milan and has offices in London, with further locations planned. Luca Ferrari is co-founder and Chief Executive Officer.
