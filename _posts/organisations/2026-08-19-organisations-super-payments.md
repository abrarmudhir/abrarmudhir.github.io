      Super Payments is a London-based payment network founded by Samir Desai CBE, co-founder of Funding Circle. It aims to remove payment-processing costs for merchants while making online shopping more rewarding for consumers.<br /><br />

      The platform supports direct bank transfers through open banking, card payments including Apple Pay and Google Pay, and its Super Credit Buy Now Pay Later offering. Super uses data and AI to support fast, secure checkout and shopper rewards; its business model includes advertising, credit, and optional payment extras rather than merchant processing fees.<br /><br />

      Super Payments Limited is a distributor of Modulr FS Limited for relevant electronic-money and payment services. Open-banking payments are powered by Yapily Connect Limited, while Super Credit is provided by Abound. The company states it has processed more than $1 billion annually, served 4 million UK customers, and raised approximately $100 million to support its growth.
