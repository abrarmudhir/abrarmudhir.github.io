      OQ describes itself as a global integrated energy group rooted in Oman and an energy-transition enabler. Its public website says the group manages energy investments across the full value chain, from exploration to distribution, and operates upstream, downstream, and alternative-energy businesses.<br /><br />

      As of August 10, 2026, OQ states that it operates in 17 countries and reaches customers in more than 80 nations. The company positions its energy-transition work around green molecules, energy assets, energy efficiency, renewable energy, and support for Oman’s national net-zero target for 2050. Its website reports more than $33 billion in total assets.<br /><br />

      OQ was created at the end of 2019 through the integration of nine Omani energy companies under Oman Oil Company and Orpic. LinkedIn lists the group as an oil-and-gas organisation headquartered in Ghubra, Muscat, with 5,001-10,000 employees. OQ’s leadership page identifies Ashraf Hamed Al Mamari as Group Chief Executive Officer.<br /><br />
