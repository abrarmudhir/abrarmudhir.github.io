      BNY is the corporate brand of The Bank of New York Mellon Corporation, a global financial-services company headquartered in New York. Its roots trace to The Bank of New York, founded in 1784; the modern corporation was created through the 2007 merger of The Bank of New York and Mellon Financial Corporation.<br /><br />

      The company sits at the heart of global capital markets, helping institutions, corporations, asset managers, banks, insurers, governments and individuals manage assets, move money and access markets. Its services include custody and fund administration, issuer services, clearing, collateral management, liquidity and financing, payments and trade, markets execution, investment management and wealth services.<br /><br />

      BNY combines financial expertise with technology across data and analytics, cloud-native platforms, artificial intelligence and digital-asset infrastructure. The company provides custody services in more than 100 markets and supports clients through the full financial lifecycle.<br /><br />
