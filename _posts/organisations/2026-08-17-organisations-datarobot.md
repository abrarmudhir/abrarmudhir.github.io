      DataRobot is an enterprise artificial-intelligence company headquartered in Boston. Its platform helps organisations build, operate, and govern AI applications and agents across cloud, hybrid, on-premises, and cross-cloud environments.<br /><br />

      Founded in 2012, DataRobot pioneered automated machine learning (AutoML) and MLOps before expanding its platform to support predictive AI, generative AI, and agentic AI. It provides tools for the full AI lifecycle, including development, deployment, observability, governance, and automated documentation.<br /><br />

      The platform is designed for enterprises operating in regulated and high-stakes environments. Its capabilities include configurable agent blueprints, integrations, model and component selection, dynamic compute orchestration, quality monitoring, access control, audit documentation, and controls for compliance requirements.<br /><br />
