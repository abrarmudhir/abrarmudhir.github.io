      Airfinity is a London-headquartered predictive analytics company serving life sciences, global health, and biosecurity teams. Its platform provides AI-powered intelligence, simulation, and forecasting across infectious disease, obesity, and cardiometabolic conditions, helping customers act on emerging signals and navigate uncertain markets.<br /><br />

      As of August 11, 2026, Airfinity's product portfolio includes FRIDA for respiratory infectious-disease forecasting, OneID for continuously updated infectious-disease and biorisk intelligence, and Nexa for drug and vaccine demand forecasting. The company also offers competitive intelligence, disease-burden modelling, and second-opinion commercial forecasts. Airfinity says its infrastructure continuously monitors hundreds of global data sources, combining predictive models with disease-expert validation.<br /><br />

      Airfinity was founded in 2015 by Rasmus Bech Hansen and Sacha Carton. Hansen is CEO and co-founder; Carton is COO and co-founder. The company says it serves more than 100 clients in over 25 countries and operates remote-first with team members distributed globally. LinkedIn classifies Airfinity Ltd as a privately held information-services company with 51-200 employees, headquartered in London.
