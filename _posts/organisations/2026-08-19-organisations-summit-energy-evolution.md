      Summit Energy Evolution Limited (SEEL) is a UK-based, wholly owned subsidiary of Sumitomo Corporation. Established following Sumitomo's 2022 exit from UK upstream oil and gas, SEEL focuses on developing partnerships for low-carbon and net-zero initiatives in the UK and beyond.<br /><br />

      The company works across the energy-transition value chain, drawing on experience in subsurface, engineering, offshore, and renewables technologies. Its focus includes hydrogen, carbon capture and storage (CCS/CCUS), renewables, infrastructure, equity and finance, and supply-chain collaboration.<br /><br />

      SEEL is involved in projects around the Bacton gas terminals in Norfolk, including the Bacton Hydrogen Hub and Bacton Thames Net Zero initiatives. Its London office is at Vintners Place, 68 Upper Thames Street, EC4V 3BJ.
