      Regulex is a London-based financial-services software company providing distribution intelligence for asset and wealth managers. Its platform brings together market signals, public filings, premium data sources, and a firm's own context to help distribution teams identify allocators in motion and prioritise outreach.<br /><br />

      The platform includes daily market briefings, market intelligence, prospecting, CRM, institutional-memory, and AI research-agent modules. Regulex states that it can be used across public and private markets and is designed to complement a firm's existing systems without requiring integrations to begin a proof of concept.<br /><br />
