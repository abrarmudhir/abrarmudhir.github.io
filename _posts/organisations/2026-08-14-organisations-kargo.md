      Kargo is an advertising technology and media company that combines creative, technology, and media to help brands create and activate digital advertising experiences across screens. Its invitation-only marketplace connects advertisers with premium inventory across connected TV, social, the open web, and emerging platforms.<br /><br />

      The company's offering includes audience targeting, online video, display, social, retail media, and CTV solutions. Kargo uses audience data, proprietary contextual signals, cross-platform measurement data, and technology for media buying and creative activation. Its Project Kera agentic AI engine is designed to bring creative, media, and campaign outcomes together in one system.<br /><br />

      Kargo is led by founder and CEO Harry Kargman. The company has offices across the United States, Australia, New Zealand, Singapore, the United Kingdom, and Ireland, including a London office at 66 Old Compton Street.<br /><br />
