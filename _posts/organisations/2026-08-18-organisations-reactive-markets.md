      Reactive Markets provides a purpose-built institutional trading network for bilateral liquidity. The company connects clients with liquidity providers across asset classes and delivers access through its user interface, APIs, and integrations with execution management systems, order management systems, and pricing engines.<br /><br />

      Founded in 2019, Reactive Markets focuses on flexible, scalable, and low-latency connectivity for institutional participants. Its public materials state that the network supports more than 35 leading liquidity providers and is used across FX, equities, and other asset classes. The company has locations in London and New York.<br /><br />

      As of August 18, 2026, Phil Morris is Chief Executive Officer. The executive leadership team also includes Chief Technology Officer Mark Aylett, Chief Design Officer Mark McDowell, and Chief Revenue Officer Henry Durrant.
